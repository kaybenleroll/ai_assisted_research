Provenance and attribution
================================

This is a small, deterministic synthetic fixture for the spectral graph theory
primer. It is deliberately shaped like the historical OpenFlights route-table
format: each row records an airline, source airport, destination airport, and
the customary route metadata fields.

The airport codes A01--A10, identifiers, airlines, and route rows were invented
for this repository. No OpenFlights records were downloaded or copied, and the
fixture is not real current aviation data, a timetable, a traffic sample, or a
measure of passenger capacity. The values are only a teaching graph with a
weak bridge between two dense synthetic regions.

Attribution: the column layout is informed by the historical OpenFlights route
data convention. This note is the provenance record for the repository-owned
synthetic rows; it does not claim that the rows originate from OpenFlights.

The example converts the directional-looking rows to an undirected graph by
canonicalising each unordered airport pair. The binary variant keeps one edge
per pair. The count variant sums repeated route records, including reverse
records, as an explicitly defined synthetic edge weight.
