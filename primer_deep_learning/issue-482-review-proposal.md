## Outline Decisions

These are provisional reader-facing decisions, not a substitute for the required boundary-by-boundary audit.

1. **Introduction → Why Deep Learning Now** (lines 3–85): retain the opening orientation before historical motivation; move notation into it. Consider combining the four short orientation child headings into a continuous guide while preserving their audience, exclusions, prerequisites and route; this trades direct links for a less fragmented start, so retain legacy anchors if headings move.
2. **Why Deep Learning Now → Neural Network Fundamentals** (85–181): retain history before mechanism. Data, compute and algorithms explain different bottlenecks; consider grouping only where the historical argument becomes repetitive, preserving the ImageNet example.
3. **Fundamentals → Training** (181–338): retain the architecture/training distinction. Pair the proposed numeric example across the boundary so the prediction becomes the input to the loss and update; retain activation and approximation limitations.
4. **Training → CNNs** (338–503): retain CNNs as their own architecture family; #516 restores its parent heading. Preserve the convolution figure, parameter count, transfer and framework examples.
5. **CNNs → RNNs** (503–663): retain the data-structure transition from images to ordered sequences; keep CNN/ViT trade-offs and the recurrent code examples.
6. **RNNs → Attention** (663–852): retain the fixed-state bottleneck as the explanatory bridge to attention; do not fold either architecture into the other. Add worked recurrence and attention calculations.
7. **Attention → LLMs** (852–949): retain the distinction between architecture and scaled training/adaptation. Preserve causal masking, BERT/GPT, tokenisation and preference-training coverage.
8. **LLMs → VAEs** (949–1027): retain a separate generative-model route; make the change of modelling goal explicit. Keep the autoencoder gap, KL, reparameterisation, code and posterior collapse.
9. **VAEs → GANs** (1027–1079): retain competing training objectives and their failure modes; preserve minimax, mode collapse and WGAN while correcting its assumptions.
10. **GANs → Diffusion** (1079–1196): retain separate iterative-denoising route; preserve forward/reverse equations, sampling, guidance, latent and text diffusion, and comparisons.
11. **Diffusion → Frameworks** (1196–1321): retain the transition from modelling mechanisms to implementation choices. Preserve three-framework examples and deployment distinctions; verify current API claims.
12. **Frameworks → Decision Guide** (1321–1369): retain framework selection separately from task/model selection. Add an applied decision thread rather than merging the two different reader tasks.
13. **Decision Guide → Further Reading → Notation** (1369–1402): keep Further Reading discoverable after synthesis. Move notation forward as required, retaining its link target. References remain available where the reader finishes, rather than being buried in the orientation.

Within each major section, inspect adjacent child boundaries during the tracked audit. Shortness alone is not a reason to merge: a subsection that answers a different practical question earns a heading, whereas successive parts of one uninterrupted calculation may be grouped. Any consolidation must preserve every substantive case, figure, equation, code example, citation and useful fragment target.
