- Reader: quantitatively literate ecological researcher or analyst who can read basic R.
- Prerequisites: algebra, logs, elementary probability, derivatives, vectors and basic regression; local refreshers accompany their use.
- Problem and motivation: observed ecological counts, proportions and positive measurements need distributions, means and uncertainty aligned with their sampling design.
- Intended outcome: construct, fit, interpret, diagnose and revise GLMs and taught extensions, distinguish conditional and predictive targets, and recognise design and model limits.
- Scope: Gaussian, binomial, Poisson, Gamma, likelihood and IRLS; inference, prediction and validation; quasi, NB, GLMM, GAM, hurdle and zero-inflated, multinomial, ordinal and Bayesian routes.
- Exclusions: complete causal, spatial, temporal, occupancy/detection and survival modelling; reference-only boundaries identify when they are needed.
- Proposed dependency-led route: design and regression, distribution/link and fitting, core responses and inference, then categorical and extensions, Bayesian synthesis and boundaries.
- Approximate depth: substantial self-contained explanations and worked cases; no word-count ceiling.
- Worked examples: fixed-design core, penalised separation, Gamma holdout, overdispersed and zero-heavy counts, random sites, smooths, categorical and Bayesian cases.
- Real-world cases: frozen Palmer penguins and explicitly package-derived Salamanders, with observational and missingness limits and data provenance in primer_glm/data/README.txt.
- Teaching-case contract: primer_glm/primer_glm.case.md, glm-case-v3.
- Worked-case trace: route-keyed table and checkpoint below; core continuous Gamma case and clearly distinguished branches.
- Core-concept depth matrix: this brief, glm-brief-v3.
- Route classification and teaching spines: route table below; each primary or alternative route has a worked mechanism, interpretation and boundary.
- Figures, tables, and equations: nine context-specific figures, model/design tables, derivations and interpreted numerical examples; release-level visual QA is pending.
- Languages and executable examples: R primary; matched bounded core, fixed-dispersion NB and penalised spline in Python and Julia; extension implementations are currently R-first. The agreed three-language main-example promise remains subject to release verification and is not silently reduced. Explicit algorithms are teaching implementations with bounded checks.
- Evidence plan: independent content review and scoped re-review, route and concept traces, source attribution, manifest checks and pinned render/visual/accessibility QA.
- Open decisions: complete current link, build, accessibility, provenance and release checks. Additional production-package implementations are not implied by toy code.

## Route classification and teaching spines

| route_id | tier | Part and reader need | Organising model, application and boundary | original IDs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| r01 | primary | I. From ecological question to regression | Sampling unit, estimand and ordinary regression; Gaussian continuous outcome with continuous and categorical covariates. Compare fitted contrasts and predictive intervals; dependent units, selection and confounding limit claims. | R01 |
| r02 | primary | II. GLM machinery | Distribution, mean, link and linear predictor; derive exponential-family moments, likelihood score, information and IRLS on Gaussian/logistic/Poisson cases. Numerical convergence and model assumptions matter. | R02 |
| r03 | primary | III. Probabilities and counts | Binomial trials/logit and Poisson events/log link; success/trial denominators, exposure offset, fitted probabilities/rates, interactions. Rare events, separation, exposure and independence failures expose limits. | R03 |
| r04 | primary | IV. Positive outcomes and inference | Gamma/log model for strictly positive skewed ecological response; likelihood, intervals, tests, prediction and residual diagnostics across families. Zeros and inverse-link averaging require care. | R04 |
| r05 | alternative | V. Dispersion and count alternatives | Overdispersed counts: quasi score with variance-scale uncertainty and separately motivated sandwich covariance, compared with a negative-binomial probability model; simulated and real count comparisons. Quasi does not specify a full likelihood; NB form and parameterisation must be explicit. | R05 |
| r06 | alternative | VI. Dependence | GLMM random intercept/slopes for clustered sampling; compare conditional/marginal predictions and prediction for new groups; few groups and weakly identified variance components limit inference. | R06 |
| r10 | alternative | VI. Flexible effects | GAM basis and penalty for nonlinear ecological gradients, with REML smoothing selection; fit, interpret and check a smooth; concurvity and extrapolation limit conclusions. | R10 |
| r11 | alternative | IV. Multicategory responses | Model unordered ecological categories using multinomial regression and ordered outcomes using an ordinal cumulative-link model; fit, interpret and compare probabilities, check proportional odds, and examine sparse categories and dependence. | R11 |
| r07 | alternative | VI. Excess zeros and observation process | Hurdle versus zero-inflated count generative stories; fit and decompose probabilities, compare predictions; zeros alone cannot identify mechanism, and imperfect detection is not automatically zero inflation. | R07 |
| r08 | alternative | VII. Bayesian modelling and decisions | Prior, likelihood and posterior for binomial and count models, prior/posterior predictive checks and predictive intervals; compare frequentist estimates under explicit priors. Separation, prior sensitivity, sampling diagnostics and computation matter. | R08 |
| r09 | reference-only | Beyond the central route | Beta and Tweedie response models, full occupancy/detection, spatial/time-series and survival modelling: explain the different response or observation structure, show the boundary of the taught methods, and give authoritative next steps; no full fitting route is promised. | R09 |
