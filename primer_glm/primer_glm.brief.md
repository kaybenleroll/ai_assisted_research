|---|---|---|---|---|

| r04 | primary | IV. Positive outcomes and inference | Gamma/log model for strictly positive skewed ecological response; likelihood, intervals, tests, prediction and residual diagnostics across families. Zeros and inverse-link averaging require care. | R04 |
| r05 | alternative | V. Dispersion and count alternatives | Overdispersed counts: quasi score with variance-scale uncertainty and separately motivated sandwich covariance, compared with a negative-binomial probability model; simulated and real count comparisons. | r06 | alternative | VI. Dependence | GLMM random intercept/slopes for clustered sampling; compare conditional/marginal predictions and prediction for new groups; few groups and weakly identified variance components limit inference. | R06 |
| r10 | alternative | VI. Flexible effects | GAM basis and penalty for nonlinear ecological gradients, with REML smoothing selection; fit, interpret and check a smooth; concurvity and extrapolation limit conclusions. | R10 |
| r11 | alternative | IV. | r07 | alternative | VI. Excess zeros and observation process | Hurdle versus zero-inflated count generative stories; fit and decompose probabilities, compare predictions; zeros alone cannot identify mechanism, and imperfect detection is not automatically zero inflation. | R07 |
| r08 | alternative | VII. Bayesian modelling and decisions | Prior, likelihood and posterior for binomial and count models, prior/posterior predictive checks and predictive intervals; compare frequentist estimates under explicit priors. Separation, prior sensitivity, sampling diagnostics and computation matter. | R08 |
| r09 | reference-only | Beyond the central route | Beta and Tweedie response models, full occupancy/detection, spatial/time-series and survival modelling: explain the different response or observation structure, show the boundary of the taught methods, and give authoritative next steps; no full fitting route is promised. | R09 |
